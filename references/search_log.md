# Literature selection log

The bibliography was assembled as a conceptual, not systematic, review.
Preference was given to peer-reviewed primary publications from ACL Anthology,
PMLR and NeurIPS proceedings. A preprint/technical report is included only when
it introduces a widely used embedding-training method and its status is stated
explicitly in the manuscript.

Search themes: sentence-embedding anisotropy and isotropy; alignment and
uniformity; whitening; hubness and local neighbourhoods; effective dimension
and rank bottlenecks; dense retrieval and query-document asymmetry;
instruction-conditioned embeddings; gradient conflict in multitask learning;
cross-task evaluation in MTEB and BEIR.

Last bibliographic verification: 2026-09-20.
